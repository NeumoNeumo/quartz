keywords: Gauge invariant / gauge DOF, collective variable, effective dimension, order parameter, low-rank matrix, dimensionless invariants.

There could possibly be only a few parameters that really matters in a complex system.

Every restriction comes with a gauge DOF, which is a generalization of the relation between symmetry and invariant.

> [!info] Why do physicists use the word *symmetry* instead of *restriction*?
> The math expression for symmetry is more tractable. Physicists often write equations(restrictions) using symmetry.

Reducing the number of parameters facilitates our math derivation

Examples:
- Low rank analysis in a [[dynamical_system|dynamical system]].
