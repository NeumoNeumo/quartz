# Definition

Idealism holds the ontological position that there is only one kind of
substance, mind. Chief among these was Bishop George Berkeley(pronounced
"Barklee". Berkeley, California was named for him with a different
pronunciation). Berkeley articulated a position variously known as
*immaterialism*, *idealism*, *psychic monism*, or *mentalistic monism*. His
doctrine is "to be is to be perceived". So only the mind itself and perception
exists in the world.
