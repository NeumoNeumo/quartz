# Definition
Object & Key Results(OKR) is an effective goal-setting framework to define measurable goals and track the outcome.
# Specification
1. Everyone needs to write down OCRs in an open and shared document.
2. Measurable OKRs help track tasks more effectively.
3. Basic OKR is mandatory and should have a target completion rate of 1 while extra OKR is voluntary whose completion rate is expected to be 0.7 so that it has a moderate ambition.
4. KR should be necessary and almost sufficient for O, which means the accomplishment of KR implies the nearly accomplishment of O
# Limitation
1. Requirement of a high standard of members: OKR frees high-quality talents from KPI designated from superiors and empower them to pursue what they think is best for the team development. However, if OKR is used in a team full of mediocre people on low levels of Maslow's hierarchy of needs, the lack of responsibility leads them to choosing easy objects.
2. Attractiveness of the team. In an OKR team, the basic salary is expected to be significantly higher than the industry average so that the members still have the motivation without extra bonus. High human capital flow is the stress that keeps the members working instead(If you are not good, go away). But the high treatment is also a challenge for a team.
3. Not all teams are suitable for OKR rather than KPI, especially for those whose goal is already explicit and quantified. And the job should be creative. You cannot expect workers in a assembly line to use OKR.
4. High maintenance cost. Plenty of work of communication from all tiers of the teams is required.
> [!hint] Reminder
> What really matters is to **seize the opportunities brought by changes in the macro-environment** and **focus on the most important factor for the team**. For instance, the most important for a restaurant is more delicious food instead of all the marketing although the latter is also necessary.
