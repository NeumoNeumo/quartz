## Classical Mechanism
Conservation of information (or the conservation of fine-grained Gibbs entropy) is equivalent to the conservation of the symplectic volume. We can derive it from the conservation of fine-grained Gibbs entropy and continuity equation.

Note: the conservation of the symplectic volume does not imply the conservation of the symplectic structure.

Gibbs entropy is conserved. But after coarse-graining, the entropy may increase. A volume of phase in a [[Complex_System|complex system]] might occupies the same volume after evolution but they are twisted and warped to everywhere in the system, occupying every coase-grained block.
