## Keys to Make a Card

1. Split complex topics into single-idea atomic cards. Every question should correspond to exactly one answer. A clear feedback avoids illusions of competence and makes your learning more focused. A counterexample: give me an example of combinational circuit.
2. Multi-Angle Questions: Reinforce learning by framing the same fact differently.
3. Put the question in a context so that you will use this knowledge in your real life. After all, why would you like to remember something that you will never use in your remaining life?

## Tips

### Active Recall

Targeted practice requires
1. well-defined specific goals
2. focus & stepping out of the comfort zone
3. feed-back

However, Swiping flashcards with passive recall has weak feedback and leave us in the comfort zone. Instead, **actively recall the answer** and correct your cognitive errors. Active recall advances a request of a **context**, e.g. place a new vocabulary in a sentence.

### Reflaction

Why are you always forgetting this card? What mistake have you made? You break one of the keys to make a card? Can you add more cards related to this knowledge to consolidate your memory? Is this card not so important? Do you need mnemonics?
