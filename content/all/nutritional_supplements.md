# Protein powder

## When

Source: https://www.healthline.com/nutrition/best-time-to-take-protein#TOC_TITLE_HDR_4

1. Consuming protein between meals raise your metabolism and reduce your
appetite.

2. Consuming protein in less than 2 hours after workout is best for building
yourself up. But resistance exercise and sufficient protein intake are more
important than timing for average people.

3. Taking protein before bed facilitates exercise performance and muscle building.

4. Dietary protein should be distributed across meals as the body has no specialized storage mechanism for it; surplus protein is oxidized for fuel. [^1]

# Antioxidant

## Astaxanthin

It has exceptional quenching behavior against singlet oxygen, hydroxyl radical
and  superoxide anion but under-performs Vitamin-C on quenching hydrogen
peroxide. according to [this page](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/396366613)

Recommended daily dosage is between 2mg to 24mg according to [this
review](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ptr.6514). No obvious side
effect at short-term daily doses of up to 100mg.

However synthetic astaxanthin is significantly inferior to algal-based
astaxanthin and my not be suitable as a human nutraceutical supplement according to [this article](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/590411019)

## Curcumin

A strong antioxidant with powerful anti-inflammatory effects. Poorly absorbable
but piperine in black peppers enhances the absorption by 2000% according to
[this study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5664031/). Average Indian
diet contains around 60-100 mg of curcumin according to [this
page](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5388087/)

## Linseed Oil

Flaxseed oil contains omega-3 in the form of ALA (alpha-linolenic acid) by
[this](https://www.health.harvard.edu/heart-health/why-not-flaxseed-oil）), which
is not directly bioavailable. In the body, it is metabolized into the more
usable forms, DHA (docosahexaenoic acid) and EPA (eicosapentaenoic acid). The
conversion rate to is rather low though.

> Studies of ALA metabolism in healthy young men indicated that approximately 8%
of dietary ALA was converted to EPA and 0%-4% was converted to docosahexaenoic
acid (DHA) . In healthy young women, approximately 21% of dietary ALA was
converted to EPA and 9% was converted to DHA.

-- from [this page](https://lpi.oregonstate.edu/mic/other-nutrients/essential-fatty-acids)

If you want a higher EPA and DHA, you may want some fish oil. [This
page](https://www.healthline.com/nutrition/flaxseed-oil-vs-fish-oil#bottom-line)
compares these two oil.

DHA is an important component of
omega-3 fatty acids in the brain according to
[this](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18023566/). Consumption of curcumin can
enhance the conversion rate of ALA to DHA by [this
paper](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/270163204_Curcumin_boosts_DHA_in_the_brain_Implications_for_the_prevention_of_anxiety_disorders).
DHA is typically obtained through fish and nuts. If you don't eat them often,
taking linseed oil with curcumin might help.

## Calcium Tablet

> 根据《中国居民膳食营养素参考摄入量（2023）》的建议，普通成人及孕妇、乳母的每日推荐钙摄入量为 800 mg；9～18 岁青少年的每日推荐钙摄入量则是 1000 mg。

However, most of the Chinese residents have insufficient daily calcium intake
through diet according to [this
paper](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0205045)

Inorganic calcium is inexpensive but the digestion requires more stomach acid.
Therefore, individuals with stomach issues should avoid it. Organic calcium is
suitable for those people. The absorption rate of different organic calcium
compounds are approximately the same according to [this
research](https://www.cnki.com.cn/Article/CJFDTotal-ZGZS200706000.htm).

When supplementing calcium, do not eat with a diet rich in phytic acid, oxalic
acid, tannic acid, and high fiber. At the same time, supplementing with vitamin
D and vitamin K2 can help with calcium absorption.

Too much calcium is detrimental to the body. FYI, check [this
post](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/662478584).

## Coenzyme Q10

CoQ10 may help support the skin, brain, and lungs and protect against chronic
diseases like cancer and type 2 diabetes according to
[Healthline](https://www.healthline.com/nutrition/coenzyme-q10#dosage). CoQ10 is
well-tolerated and is not associated with any serious side effects. Its two
forms, ubiquinol and ubiquinone, do not show significant difference between
ubiquinol and ubiquinone regarding absorption in the [current
study](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7146408/). Studies related
to heart health typically use doses of 100 to 400 mg per day. CoQ10 is a
fat-soluble which makes it more absorbable after meals.

## NAD+

Actually, according to [this post](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/222310716), NAD+
internal circulation is more important than symply supplementing NAD+
precursors. Measures to boost NAD+ cycle includes sports, fasting and reduced
carbo intake.

NAD+ supplement is beneficial for longevity. However, directly intaking NAD+
cannot effectively increase the NAD+ in your body. The mainstream to
supplementing NAD+ is through NAD+ precursors incluing VB3(including 烟酸(NA),
烟酰胺(NAM), 烟酰胺核苷(NR)) and NMN.

According to [this post](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/147931954), niacin is the
best. (16 mg NE/day)

## Quercetin

As usual, basic information about quercetin can be found on
[Healthline](https://www.healthline.com/nutrition/quercetin#benefits). Moreover,
quercetin inhibits CD38  that consumes NAD+. The bioavailability is relatively
low(<10%) according to [this
research](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0924224421006506).
VC and bromelian enhance its absorption.

Typical daily dosage: 500-1000mg.
