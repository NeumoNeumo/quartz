title: Mackey's theorem

Let $H,K\leq G$, and let $\psi$ be a representation of $H$. Then

$$
\operatorname{Res}^{G}_{K}\operatorname{Ind}^{G}_{H}\psi
\cong
\bigoplus_{s\in K\backslash G/H}
\operatorname{Ind}^{K}_{K\cap sHs^{-1}}
\left(\psi^{s}\big|_{K\cap sHs^{-1}}\right),
$$

where

$$
\psi^{s}(h)=\psi(s^{-1}hs),
$$

and $K\backslash G/H$ denotes a set of representatives for the double cosets of $K$ and $H$ in $G$.
