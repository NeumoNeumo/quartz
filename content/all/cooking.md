## Tips:
### Salt

1. **Salt pasta water**(10 gram per liter of water) to enhance the firmness and elasticity of the noodles [为什么面团加盐会让面条变得更劲道？ - 开尔文的回答 - 知乎](https://www.zhihu.com/question/541050505/answer/2633245902)  and prevent them from becoming overly sticky and flavor. But excessive salt in food, due to its high osmotic pressure, can denature proteins, which is not only detrimental to the formation of gluten networks, but also to fermentation.
2. Add salt in oil prevents food from sticking to the pot because of the accelerated protein denaturation.
### Alkali
1. Enhances noodle elasticity by promoting disulfide bonds, but may impart an alkaline taste if overused.  
2. Neutralizes acidity in fermented dough, improving flavor.  
3. Decreases ingredients by removing excess oil.  
4. Increases niacin bioavailability in corn, preventing pellagra.  
5. Promotes gelatinization by disrupting hydrogen bonds in starch granules.
> [!warning]
> Overuse destroys vitamins B1, B2, and C and hinders mineral absorption. Use sparingly.

## Misc
1. Paper towel can be used to
	- soak up excessive oil or water
	- wrap food in a microwave after soaked to provide a wet atmosphere
2. Higher temperatures will cause the egg mixture to coagulate faster, resulting in larger pieces of egg. So if you want the egg to coat the surface of the noodles, lower the temperature.
3. Better scrambled eggs:
	- Stir eggs thoroughly
	- Add some warm water
	- Oil prevents from sticking pots. [Spoiled water also functions as an isolating layer](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/63254505).
4. **Adding salt, eggs or baking soda** can make the noodles more elastic. [[cooking#Salt|Why salt?]] Eggs contain a large amount of proteins with thiol groups(巯基). The proteins in eggs can participate in the formation of gluten networks, making the gluten network more stable. [[cooking#Alkali|Why alkali?]]
