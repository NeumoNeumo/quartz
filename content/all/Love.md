# Early stage：

《我想谈恋爱！科学脱单指南》: focus on falling in love with others and how to establish a
high-quality interactive communication in the early stage of a romance.

It's upgrading the relationship but playing together that is the key point of
dates. In a date, you evaluate the other one's personality, how they deal with
other people, IQ/EQ, value system and their outlooks towards the world, life,
love, e.t.c to envisage the possibility of get along with each other in harmony
and supporting each other.

It is recommended to **hold hands** in the **third** dates.
[Why](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/483695096) Any intimacy behavior is testing
the relationship between two people, enhancing the bond if the intimacy level is
above some degree, ruining the bond if below. Note that it's not a dogma that
you have to stick on.

The place of the first date should be quiet so that you can communicate easily. Do not stay in the same place for a long time which is boring. Changing the scenes also creates more impressive and interesting shared experience.
