The duration of the experience does not matter to memory. Instead, memory is more sensitive to KEY moments(representativeness heuristic) and the end the the experience(recency bias). [^1]

> Memory doesn't make film, it makes photographs. -- Milan Kundera

例如在度蜜月的时候就要去创造强烈的情感印记。例如共同面对一点难度，然后一起获得释放。具体而言，可以先坐船去一个偏远小岛，有一点未知感，最后看到日落和海。一天的体验曲线可以是舒适开场->小挑战->情绪峰值->放松->温柔收尾，睡前可以让双方各自说出“今天最想记住的一个画面”，因为这在主动整理记忆。具体可以问AI。

[^1]: [All’s well that ends (and peaks) well? A meta-analysis of the peak-end rule and duration neglect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0749597822000334)
