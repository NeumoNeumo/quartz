Dominated convergence theorem

*Discrete version*: 
1. $\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}f_n(k) = f(k)$
2. $|f_n(k)|\le g(k)$
3. $\sum g(k) < \infty$

Then $\lim_{n\rightarrow \infty}\sum_{k=1}^\infty f_n(k) = \sum_{k=1}^\infty f(k)$

*Continuous version*:
1. $\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}f_n(x) = f(x)$ a.e.
2. $|f_n(x)|\le g(x)$
3. $\int_{I} g(x) dx < \infty$

Then $\lim_{n\rightarrow \infty}\int_{I} f_n(x) dx =\int_{I} f(k) dx$
