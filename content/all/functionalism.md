# Definition
## Two Kinds

The first kind is *keys* which are determined by their functions instead of
material. They can be wooden key, iron key, alloyed key, digital key, etc.
Anything that opens a lock is a key. The other kind is *diamonds* which are
determined by their material instead of functions. The notorious alternative,
cubic zirconia, is also hard and transparent and has similar optical property.
But It's not diamond at the end of the day. Being a diamond means the thing
itself. 
## Functionalism

*Functionalism claims that the mental states are identified by what they does
rather than what they are made of.*  Namely, the kind of mental states is the
*key* kind. For functionalism, being is doing. In one version or another,
functionalism remains the most widely endorsed theory about the nature of mental
states among contemporary theorists.
## Concept Differentiation

1. functionalism vs [[mind-body_dualism]]: According to the latter, mind
consists of a special kind of thinking substance, the *res cogitans*.

2. functionalism vs mind-brain identity theory (monism): The latter believes
that mental states are special kinds of biological states of the brain which is
more like diamond kinds. The relation between mind and a brain is one-to-one.
For instance, there exists a certain physical structure in brains that
represents being pain in all kinds of brains including mammalian brains,
reptilian brains, mollusc’s brains.

3. functionalism vs B. F. Skinner’s behaviorism: The latter identifies the
metal states solely by the behavior in response to stimuli, while the former
believe a finer-grained internal structure, that is distinct psychological
states could leads to the same behavior.
