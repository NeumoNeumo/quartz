- Have you considered the linear form? What's the eigenspace/eigenvalue?
- Low rank analysis. Find the effective low dimentional subspace simplifies the dynamics, e.g. when $A$ and $B$ is low rank in $\dot x = Ax + Bu$. A concrete example in [Chapter 4.1 of this paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.13567v2).
- Tikhonov's theorem. Freeze part of the complex system, e.g. [letting $W = 0$ in Chapter 4.2 of this paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.13567v2).
