# Definition
Descartes's dualism, also known as substance dualism, believes mind and body are
composed of material substances and immaterial substances respectively, both
created by God. His dualism is interactive dualism which means mind and body
interacts as separate parts to work. 
# Cartesian impasse
The organ to connect the physical and the non-physical is pineal gland according
to Descartes. But how the interaction is possible remains unknown to Descarte.
The sticking-point is referred to as the Cartesian impasse by the philosopher
G.N.A. Vesey.
# Another belief
[[monism|Monism]] says there is only one substance in the world.
